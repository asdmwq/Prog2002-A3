Our Purpose

Connecting caring people with local causes

HeartBridge Charity Foundation gives everyone in Liuzhou a simple way to discover charity activities, understand why each event matters, and choose a way to take part — from a morning fun run beside the Liujiang River to an evening gala dinner.

Every event on this site is run with a clear purpose, and every yuan raised is tracked against a public goal so supporters can see exactly how their contribution helps.

  • ✓ Education and weekend clubs for children in local neighbourhoods
  • ✓ Conservation of the Liujiang River and its riverside parks
  • ✓ Community wellbeing, mentoring and family support
